# Kitchen Equipment Sales Analysis
### Sales Performance Dashboard | Google BigQuery & Looker Studio

## Project Overview
This project analyzes kitchen equipment sales data to evaluate sales performance, identify top-performing products and categories, explore monthly sales trends, and examine geographic sales distribution.

SQL queries were developed in Google BigQuery to explore and aggregate the data. The analysis was then presented through an interactive Looker Studio dashboard to support data-driven business insights.

## Key Insights
1. Total sales from completed transactions reached IDR 5.27 billion.

2. The dataset contains 10,000 unique orders.

3. Cooking Equipment was the leading category, contributing approximately 79.5% of completed sales revenue.

4. Home Espresso Coffee Machine was the top-performing product, generating approximately IDR 640.2 million in completed sales.

5. Monthly sales peaked in August 2025, reaching approximately IDR 483.0 million.
